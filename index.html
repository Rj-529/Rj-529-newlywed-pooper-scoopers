pre-nup faq

Questions before you commit

Do I have to sign a contract?

No vows you can't break. Service runs week to week — cancel or pause anytime, no cancellation fee.

How much does it cost?

$24 per visit weekly, $20 per visit twice-weekly ($40/week total), or $30 per visit every other week, for one dog on a typical South Tampa lot. Each additional dog is +$4/week.

What happens if it rains on my visit day?

If weather makes us skip your yard, we'll come back and make it up within 48 hours — no missed visits, no extra charge.

Do you service my neighborhood?

We're currently booking across South Tampa. Send us your address and we'll confirm you're on the route.
